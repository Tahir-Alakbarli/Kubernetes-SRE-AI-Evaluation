# INC-7521 — Checkout degradation after traffic normalized

Severity: SEV-1

Checkout confirmations began timing out during a short demand spike. Incoming traffic has returned to its usual level, but the service continues to show elevated latency and intermittent failures. Kubernetes reports the application Pods as Ready. Customer operations has not identified the causal mechanism.

Restore reliable checkout behavior under normal and burst traffic. Keep the cluster's standard operational and security rules in force. Investigate independently using the live cluster and the available operational documentation.
