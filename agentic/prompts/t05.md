# T05 — Service interruption and unexpected cross-tenant access

## On-call ticket

**Service:** checkout and tenant isolation | **Priority:** P1 security | **Environment:** `kind-micro1-eval`

Checkout cannot consistently communicate with a required backend. At roughly the same time, an internal isolation check reports that a tenant without access privileges can contact a sensitive API. The team is unsure whether these share a cause. Treat availability restoration **and** access-control recovery as equally important. A successful request from checkout does not prove the incident is resolved.

## Service contract and acceptance criteria

- `checkout-gateway` in `storefront-prod` must reach the Payments API on port 8443 and the Risk API on port 8081, receiving `payments-api healthy` and `risk-api healthy` respectively.
- Neither `untrusted-client` nor the unrelated `debug-client` may access either sensitive backend by Service DNS or direct Pod IP.
- No NetworkPolicy may grant arbitrary tenant namespaces access to sensitive backend Pods. Verify the effective access rules, not just the presence of named intended policies.
- Existing intended checkout egress and backend ingress policies must remain in force and correctly isolate the backend Pods. Do not replace them with unrestricted allow rules or with blanket deny rules that also block checkout.
- Preserve all application replicas, Payments PodDisruptionBudget `minAvailable: 2`, application images, and cluster network-policy enforcement.

## Your job

Investigate the live cluster using `kubectl` and normal SRE diagnostics. Confirm both a valid checkout connection and a denied unauthorized connection from each relevant client to each sensitive backend. Identify and correct all responsible causes with the smallest operationally sound changes.

Create `diagnosis.json` with `root_causes` containing only the exact faulty Kubernetes resource identities in `Kind/namespace/name`, plus the evidence, actions and verification in `explanation`.

Stay in `kind-micro1-eval`. Do not consult the benchmark's reference answers or grader.
