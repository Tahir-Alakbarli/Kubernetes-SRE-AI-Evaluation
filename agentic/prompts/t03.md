# T03 — Application rollout fails to converge

## On-call ticket

**Service:** settlement processing | **Priority:** P1 | **Environment:** `kind-micro1-eval`

The `settlement-worker` service in namespace `worker-prod` is running an older revision. The latest approved rollout has not converged. Operations dashboards contain general capacity alerts, but no root cause has been confirmed. Existing Pods are serving traffic, and restarting them without a recovery plan would risk availability.

Investigate the scheduler and rollout behavior rather than assuming the first alert is the cause.

## Service contract and acceptance criteria

- Exactly three `settlement-worker` Pods must be updated, Ready and available at the current Deployment generation.
- The only eligible workload nodes are `micro1-eval-worker` and `micro1-eval-worker2`. The control-plane node is prohibited.
- Use the existing worker-role labeling scheme to constrain placement. Maintain required role-based worker placement in the corrected Pod template, using the existing node labels, and distribute the workload across **both** eligible workers.
- The Deployment must retain replicas=3 and rolling-update `maxUnavailable: 0`. Preserve the settlement PodDisruptionBudget `minAvailable: 2`.
- Do not change node labels, taints, cluster size, or cluster-wide quotas; do not remove placement isolation, reduce replicas, or bypass the disruption policy. The namespace is authorized for a maximum of 384Mi aggregate container memory limits, including the extra Pod needed during a no-unavailability rollout. You may adjust the namespace quota within that budget.
- The unrelated Payments service and tenant isolation must remain healthy.

## Your job

Use `kubectl` to inspect scheduling events, ReplicaSet and Deployment history, node metadata, Pods and placement rules. Repair the complete cause of the rollout failure. Verify the final updated revision and Pod distribution.

Create `diagnosis.json` with `root_causes` containing the minimal exact faulty resources in `Kind/namespace/name`, evidence, changes and verification in `explanation`.

Work only against `kind-micro1-eval`; do not open graders or author reference files.
