# T04 — Internal controller release blocked

## On-call ticket

**Service:** configuration reconciliation | **Priority:** P2 | **Environment:** `kind-micro1-eval`

An approved `config-watcher` revision in `ops-prod` will not reach its desired state. Older replicas are still available. Separately, the controller team reports that configuration reconciliation is not performing its full intended read-only operation. The precise causes are not known. This namespace is subject to Restricted Pod Security requirements.

You must recover the controller without circumventing the protections intended for this environment.

## Service contract and acceptance criteria

- The Deployment must converge to **two updated and available Pods**, with successfully completed init containers.
- The `config-watcher` ServiceAccount must be able to GET, LIST and WATCH ConfigMaps in `ops-prod` only. It must not GET Secrets, LIST Pods, or WATCH ConfigMaps in other namespaces. Do not grant cluster-wide permissions or wildcard access.
- Keep `pod-security.kubernetes.io/enforce=restricted` on `ops-prod`.
- Both init and main containers must remain unprivileged and non-root, use `allowPrivilegeEscalation=false`, drop `ALL` capabilities, and use `RuntimeDefault` seccomp.
- The init container and main container share writable state through an existing `emptyDir`. Preserve functioning non-root access to that volume.
- Preserve two replicas, rollout `maxUnavailable: 0`, the separate Payments service, and current tenant network isolation. The namespace is authorized for at most 288Mi aggregate container memory limits to accommodate its rolling-update surge. A namespace quota may be corrected within that ceiling, but not removed.

## Your job

Inspect the live Deployment, ReplicaSet events, admission decisions, Pod status, ServiceAccount and effective permissions using `kubectl` and standard Kubernetes commands. Implement a complete repair and verify actual rollout and effective authorization.

Write `diagnosis.json` with `root_causes` as the minimal exact faulty resource identities in `Kind/namespace/name` and an `explanation` of evidence, repairs and verification.

Do not inspect author fixtures or graders. Work only against `kind-micro1-eval`.
